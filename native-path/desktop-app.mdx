# Morpheus Desktop App — A Beginner's Walkthrough

This guide walks you through the Morpheus Desktop App from a blank computer to your
first AI reply, one screen at a time. Since v7.13.0 the app installs as
**MorpheusUI** — same app, new name and a rebuilt interface.

**What you'll do:** Download → Install → Launch → Set up a wallet → Add a little
funding → Pick a model → Open a session → Chat → Understand what happens next.

**Time:** about 10 minutes, plus however long you spend chatting.

---

## Before You Start

**What Morpheus is, in one sentence:** it's a way to chat with AI models that run on
other people's computers around the world, instead of one company's servers.

**What you'll need:**

- Windows 10/11 (this guide shows Windows; macOS and Linux installers are on the same
  releases page)
- An internet connection (skip this entirely with the free offline local model, see Step 4's tip)
- To use a network model: **at least 5 MOR** (the minimum to open a session) and a
  little ETH on Base
- About 10 minutes

## Walkthrough

<Steps>
  <Step title="Download & Install">
    1. Go to the [Releases page](https://github.com/MorpheusAIs/Morpheus-Lumerin-Node/releases),
       open the **latest stable release** (skip anything tagged `-test` — those are
       pre-releases), and download `win-x64-morpheus-app-<version>.exe`.

       On a Mac, use `mac-arm64-morpheus-app-<version>.dmg` (Apple Silicon) or
       `mac-x64-…dmg` (Intel). On Linux, use the `.AppImage` for your architecture.

    2. Run it — Windows may show a SmartScreen warning; **More info → Run anyway**.

    **Installed.**
  </Step>

  <Step title="First Launch">
    Open **MorpheusUI** from the Start menu.

    <Tip>
      Want a quick look around first? **Quick start** in the sidebar walks you through
      Wallet, Chat, Workspace and Settings in four short steps.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set Up Your Wallet">
    1. Set a password.

    2. **Create** (new user) or **Recover** (existing seed phrase).

    3. If creating: you'll see a **seed phrase**.

       <Frame>
         <img src="/native-path/images/desktop-app-01-seed-phrase.png" alt="Morpheus Desktop App seed phrase screen shown during wallet creation" />
       </Frame>

       <Warning>
         Never share this. Never screenshot and post it. Write it down on paper.
       </Warning>

       For the full picture on backup methods and spotting scams, see
       [Wallet Setup & Seed Phrase Safety](/native-path/wallet-setup).

    <Tip>
      **Recovered a wallet and it shows 0 MOR?** One seed phrase can hold several
      accounts. Use the **Account** dropdown at the top of the Wallet tab, next to
      your address, before assuming anything is wrong — your funds are often on a
      different account.
    </Tip>

    **Wallet exists, currently empty — expected.**
  </Step>

  <Step title="Fund Your Wallet">
    | | For | Think of it as |
    |---|---|---|
    | ETH (Base) | Gas | The toll for using the road |
    | MOR | Using the network | What you're here for |

    1. Wallet tab shows `0 MOR / 0 ETH`.

    2. Send MOR + ETH, or use Receive's QR code. You need **at least 5 MOR** to open
       a session, and only a little ETH — fees on Base are tiny.

    **Funded.**

    <Tip>
      Don't have MOR or Base ETH yet? See
      [Getting MOR & ETH on Base](/native-path/funding-your-wallet) for how to
      actually acquire and send both.
    </Tip>

    <Tip>
      **Free path, no funding needed:** in Chat, pick the bundled **local model** —
      runs entirely on your machine.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose a Model and Provider">
    1. In Chat, click **Change Model**. Search the list by name or tag, or sort it by
       price per second. Pick any model other than **Local Model**. A model with no
       live provider right now shows a note instead of a price.

    2. Next you'll see the **providers** offering that model, each with a price and a
       score. The top-scored provider is selected by default — a sensible choice.

    3. Leave the payment option on **Stake** (the default). **Direct Pay** locks the
       same amount.

    The Fee and Stake shown in the Models tab are the model's own listing bond — not
    what you'll pay to use it.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open a Session (Staking, Explained)">
    A **stake** is a refundable deposit, not a payment — like a hotel deposit.

    1. Before you confirm, read the confirm dialog. It shows two numbers, a few
       hundred times apart:
       - **MOR to be locked** — your stake. Refundable; locked to guarantee the
         session, not spent.
       - **Compute cost** — what the session actually costs.

    2. Confirm. The Wallet tab shows available MOR dropped by the stake amount, and
       **Staked Balance** up by the same amount — exactly what the dialog said.

       <Frame>
         <img src="/native-path/images/desktop-app-02-wallet-page-right-after-staking.png" alt="MorpheusUI Wallet tab right after opening a session: 11.2552 MOR available, Staked Balance 4.78 MOR, and the Account dropdown next to the wallet address at the top right" />
       </Frame>

       <Note>
         This is expected — your MOR is locked, not spent. Clicked Open twice by
         accident? The app reuses the session that's already open instead of locking
         your stake a second time.
       </Note>

    3. Back in Chat, the session card at the top shows the session ID, the MOR staked
       for it, and a countdown timer. The **Workspace** button next to it only becomes
       available once a session is open.

    **Session open.**
  </Step>

  <Step title="Chat & Run Inference">
    Type a message and send it. Under each answer you'll see its token count and cost
    — for example `5 in · 9 out · <0.0001 MOR` — so you can see what every reply costs
    as you go. That's the compute cost at work, and it's tiny next to the stake.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/native-path/images/desktop-app-03-open-session.png" alt="MorpheusUI Chat with an open session: the session card shows the session ID, 4.78 MOR staked and a countdown timer; below the model's reply is its token count and cost, 5 in, 9 out, under 0.0001 MOR" />
    </Frame>

    **You've used decentralized AI.**
  </Step>

  <Step title="What Happens When You're Done">
    - **The session runs until the timer ends.** About a minute later, your node
      submits the close transaction. MorpheusUI v7.14.0 doesn't offer a button to end
      a session early, so plan for it to run its full length.
    - **What comes back at close:** only the *unused* part of your stake. Because the
      session ran its full length, that's just a sliver.
    - **The rest is held until the end of that UTC day** — anywhere from minutes to
      about 24 hours. Then it needs a **claim** before it's back in your spendable
      balance. This is current network behavior, not a bug in your setup.
    - **There's no claim button in the app yet.** See
      [Claiming Your Stake Back](/native-path/understanding-session-economics#claiming-your-stake-back)
      for how to claim.

    For the full picture — verified on-chain examples, why this happens, and how to
    check it yourself — see [Understanding Session Economics](/native-path/understanding-session-economics).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Understanding the App: A Tour of Each Section

- **Wallet** — balances, address, staking activity. Switch between wallets and
  accounts, and send MOR or ETH. The **Staking Dashboard** link here opens Lumerin's
  LMR → MOR staking pools in your browser — a separate product. It can't claim back
  stake from your sessions.
- **Chat** — talk to models, manage sessions.
- **Workspace** — a mode for multi-step tasks with a plan view and run timer.
  Available only while a session is open.
- **Models** — browse the network's registry.
- **Agents** — task-oriented AI beyond simple chat.
- **Provider Hub** — register your own machine to *earn* MOR (a different flow — see
  the [Provider guide](/native-path/provider)).
- **Instructions** — custom instructions sent with every Chat message, to every
  model: a tone, a format, a language, or anything a model should always know about
  you. Stored on your computer only (up to 4,000 characters); leave it empty to turn
  it off.
- **Settings** — preferences, network config.
- **Quick start** — a short in-app tour of the main sections (Wallet, Chat,
  Workspace, Settings). You can skip it at any point.
- **Help & docs** — docs and support.

## Troubleshooting & Common Questions

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| My MOR dropped a lot more than the session cost — lost money? | No — see Step 6. Refundable stake, not a payment. The confirm dialog shows both numbers before you open. |
| My session ended but I still don't see my full MOR back | Expected — see Step 8. The used part is held until the end of that UTC day, then needs a claim ([how to claim](/native-path/understanding-session-economics#claiming-your-stake-back)). |
| I recovered my wallet and it shows 0 MOR | Check the **Account** dropdown at the top of the Wallet tab — your funds are probably on a different account from the same seed phrase. |
| I clicked Open Session twice | No harm done — the app reuses the session that's already open instead of locking your stake twice. |
| I can't find a way to end my session early | There isn't one in MorpheusUI v7.14.0 — the session closes on its own when the timer runs out. |
| Can the Wallet tab's Staking Dashboard claim my held stake? | No — it's Lumerin's LMR → MOR staking pools, a separate product. Claim held session stake as described in [Claiming Your Stake Back](/native-path/understanding-session-economics#claiming-your-stake-back). |
| Workspace is greyed out or won't open | Open a session first — Workspace only works while a session is active. |
| The Models tab "Stake" doesn't match my session's stake | Different things — model listing bond vs. your session stake. |

## Glossary

- **MOR** — token used to access AI models.
- **ETH (Base)** — small gas fee token.
- **Stake** — refundable deposit to open a session. The unused part returns at close;
  the used part is held until the end of that UTC day, then needs a manual claim
  (Step 8).
- **Session** — a time-boxed window to chat with a model.
- **Provider** — someone else's computer serving the model.
