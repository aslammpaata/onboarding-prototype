Every Native path guide — Desktop App, `morctl`, headless router, Provider — has you

four times, they all link here. Read this once; it applies no matter which client
you're using.

## What a Wallet Actually Is

A crypto wallet isn't an account with a company — it's a **key pair you control**.
The practical version:

- Your **seed phrase** (usually 12 or 24 words) is the master key. Anyone who has it
  has full, permanent, irreversible control of everything in that wallet.
- Your wallet **address** (the long `0x...` string) is safe to share — it's how
  people send you MOR or ETH.
- Your app **password** (Desktop App, browser extension, etc.) only unlocks the app on
  *this device*. It is not a backup and does not protect your funds if someone else
  has your seed phrase.

<Warning>
  **No company, support agent, or piece of software can reset a lost seed phrase.**
  There's no "forgot password" flow. If it's lost and you don't have a backup, the
  funds in that wallet are gone permanently. If someone else obtains it, they have the
  same total control you do — instantly, and irreversibly.
</Warning>

## Creating or Recovering a Wallet

<Steps>
  <Step title="Decide: new wallet, or an existing one?">
    - **New to Morpheus?** Create a new wallet — most Native path clients (like the
      [Desktop App](/native-path/desktop-app)) generate one for you during setup.
    - **Already have a wallet from elsewhere** (MetaMask, a hardware wallet, another
      app)? You can recover it using its existing seed phrase instead of creating a
      second one.
  </Step>

  <Step title="If creating: write down the seed phrase immediately">
    You'll see a screen showing 12 or 24 words, in order. This is the only time most
    clients will display it in full.

    - Write it on paper, by hand, in order.
    - Do **not** screenshot it.
    - Do **not** paste it into a notes app, password manager's "secure note," email
      draft, or cloud storage — even briefly.
    - Do **not** type it into any website, ever, for any reason.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Store the paper copy somewhere durable and offline">
    A drawer, a safe, or a safety deposit box all work. The goal is: it survives your
    device breaking, and it's not accessible from the internet.

    <Tip>
      Consider a second copy stored in a different physical location, in case the
      first is lost to fire, flood, or theft. Some people etch or stamp their phrase
      into metal for extra durability — overkill for small amounts, worth considering
      for larger ones.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Confirm the phrase if the app asks you to">
    Most wallets make you re-enter the words (or a few of them) to confirm you copied
    them down correctly, before letting you continue. This is a real check — if it
    fails, re-copy from the original screen rather than guessing.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set a local app password">
    This is separate from the seed phrase. It only protects the app on this specific
    device from casual access (e.g., someone picking up your laptop) — it does **not**
    replace or protect the seed phrase itself, and it does not sync anywhere.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What Your Seed Phrase Can and Can't Do

| | True or False |
|---|---|
| Anyone with my seed phrase can move my funds, from anywhere, without my device | **True** |
| My seed phrase can be reset like a password if I lose it | **False** — there is no reset |
| My app password protects my funds even if someone gets my seed phrase | **False** — the seed phrase alone is enough |
| I need my seed phrase to receive funds | **False** — only your public address is needed for that |
| Support staff or Morpheus employees might legitimately ask for my seed phrase | **False, always** — see below |

---

## Signs of a Seed Phrase Scam

<Warning>
  **Nobody legitimate will ever ask for your seed phrase.** Not Morpheus support, not
  a Discord moderator, not a "wallet sync" or "wallet verification" tool, not a
  browser extension popup claiming your wallet needs re-authentication. If anything
  asks for it outside the one-time backup screen during wallet creation, it's a scam —
  full stop.
</Warning>

Common patterns worth recognizing:

- A DM or support ticket asking you to "verify" your wallet by entering your phrase
  into a form or bot.
- A site that looks like a bridge, faucet, or dashboard, prompting for your seed
  phrase instead of just asking you to connect a wallet (connecting never requires
  typing the phrase anywhere).
- Urgency ("your funds are at risk, act now") paired with a request for the phrase.

If you're ever unsure whether a prompt is legitimate, the safe default is: **don't
enter it.** A genuine wallet action (sending funds, approving a contract) never
requires typing your seed phrase into anything after the initial backup step.

## Which Client Actually Holds Your Wallet?

- **[Desktop App](/native-path/desktop-app)** — generates and stores the wallet for
  you, with the guided backup flow described above.
- **[`morctl`](/native-path/morctl) / [Headless router](/native-path/headless-developer)**
  — expect you to already have a wallet and provide its credentials (private key or
  cookie file) directly; they don't walk you through creating one.
- **[Provider setup](/native-path/provider)** — same as above: bring an existing
  wallet, funded and ready, before you start.

If you're setting up any of the CLI or headless paths and don't have a wallet yet,
create one with the Desktop App first, then use its credentials with the other tools.

## Troubleshooting

| Situation | What to do |
|---|---|
| I lost my seed phrase and don't have a backup | There is no recovery. If the wallet still has a balance, moving forward means creating a new wallet and, if possible, transferring remaining access before it's lost entirely. There is nothing Morpheus support can do to restore it. |
| I think I typed my seed phrase into something I shouldn't have | Assume the wallet is compromised. Immediately create a new wallet and move any remaining funds to it before someone else does — the address doing this first wins. |
| I forgot my local app password, but still have my seed phrase | Not a problem — reinstall or reset the app and recover using your seed phrase. This is exactly what the seed phrase is for. |
| Someone in Discord/support is asking for my seed phrase to "help" | That's not a legitimate request. Don't provide it, and report the account. |

## Glossary

- **Seed phrase** — 12 or 24 words that fully control a wallet. The master key.
- **Private key** — the cryptographic key your seed phrase generates; functionally
  equivalent in what it controls.
- **Wallet address** — the public `0x...` string, safe to share, used to receive
  funds.
- **App password** — a local unlock for one device's app instance; not a backup, not
  a substitute for the seed phrase.
